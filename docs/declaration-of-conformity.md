# EU declaration of conformity (template)

**This is a template. It is not signed and it is not in force.** No EU declaration of conformity
is issued, and no CE marking is affixed, before 11 December 2027, the date from which the conformity
assessment and the CE marking apply to `hodeishield` (Article 71(2) of the Cyber Resilience Act,
[Regulation (EU) 2024/2847](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng)).

The template follows Annex V of the Regulation. Fields in angle brackets, such as `<version>`, are
filled in when a declaration is issued. How it is issued, who signs it, when it is reviewed and how
the CE marking is shown are in
[technical-documentation.md](technical-documentation.md#procedure-declaration-and-ce-marking).

From 11 December 2027, the declaration of each release is the copy of this file in that release's
tag, with the fields filled in and without this introduction:
`https://github.com/Hodeitek/hodeishield-cli/blob/v<version>/docs/declaration-of-conformity.md`.
A simplified declaration (Annex VI) may point to that address.
